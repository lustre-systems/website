A visit, start to finish

Six steps. Each one short enough to do while the patient waits.

The order is the one the practice already works in. Anything slow, like typing up the doctor's notes, waits for a quiet moment and never holds up a payment.

  1. 10:021

    Arrives and checks in

    One tap on today's list. A walk-in is booked and checked in in one step.

  2. 10:032

    Waits

    The day view shows who's waiting, who's in the chair and who's next.

  3. 10:083

    Sees the doctor

    The doctor writes on paper as usual. It can go into the record later.

  4. 10:404

    Back at the desk

    Confirm what was done. Prices come from the doctor's list and can be changed for this visit.

  5. 10:425

    Pays, all or part

    Cash, Visa or InstaPay. Whatever's left carries onto the patient's balance.

  6. 10:436

    Books the next visit

    She picks the time and length. A slot that's taken simply won't save.

The money

Know what came in without reading the book.

  • Charged, collected, still dueFor today, this week, this month or the year. Money paid late for an older visit is counted separately.
  • Paying in parts is normalA balance is just a balance. It carries from visit to visit and shows on the patient's record.
  • Old prices stay oldChanging a price today never changes what an earlier visit cost.
Three things it won't do

It stays out of the way.

It won't put your records in someone else's cloud

Records live on a computer in your clinic. An encrypted copy goes each night to the doctor's own Google Drive.

It won't message patients by itself

It writes the reminder and opens the chat. She presses send. The clinic's number is never at risk for automated messages.

It won't book two patients into one slot

The clinic's database refuses an overlapping booking outright, whatever screen it comes from.
