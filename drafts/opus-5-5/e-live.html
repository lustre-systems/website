Your records stay in your clinic.

On a computer at the practice, with an encrypted copy each night in the doctor's own Google Drive.

It never messages patients by itself.

It writes the reminder. A person presses send, so your number never gets flagged.

It never books two patients in one slot.

Refused by the database itself, not by a screen that could get it wrong.

We set it up. Then we teach it.

You won't be handed an app and left to work it out.

  1. We install it

    The clinic computer, the secretary's phone and the doctor's phone, set up and connected.

  2. We put your clinic in it

    Your prices, procedures, branches, appointment lengths and reminder wording, ready before day one.

  3. We train your team

    We sit with your secretary and doctor and go through a real day: booking, check-in, payment, reminders.
